Questions, answered.

Who can create an expo?

Only organizers. Exhibitors and attendees register themselves, while organizer accounts are created by an administrator.

How are passwords protected?

Passwords are hashed with bcrypt and sessions use signed tokens. If you forget yours, we email a one-time reset link that expires.

Can two exhibitors book the same booth?

No. A booking is saved as one atomic update, and every screen shows the new status in real time.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. The site is responsive and runs in Chrome, Firefox, Safari and Edge, on desktop and mobile.

What happens to my data?

You choose what to share and give consent when you register. We follow GDPR practices, and you can ask for your data to be deleted.

Is payment included?

Not in version 1. Booth prices are shown, and online payment is planned for a later release.
